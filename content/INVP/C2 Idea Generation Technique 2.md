## Concept Fan
A way of finding different approaches to a problem after *rejecting* all obvious solutions. It has the principle of "taking one step back" to get a broader perspective.
## Fish Bone
Featuring **cause and effect analysis**, Fishbone is about identifying all the likely causes of problems before coming up with solutions.
## SCAMPER
- S = Simplify or Substitute?
- C = Combine or Condense?
- A = Adapt or Alter?
- M = Modify, Magnify or Miniaturize?
- P = Put to other uses?
- E = Eliminate or Expand?
- R = Reverse (roles etc.) or rearrange (patterns, etc.)?
## Stormdraining
5 rules:
1. **Everything is on the Table**
   Nothing is sacred, every single item can go down the drain.
2. **Delete is the Default**
   
3. **Build on Other People's Deletions**
4. **Make it fun**
5. **When you delete something, really delete it**
