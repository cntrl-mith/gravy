## Full Life Cycle Use case
- How will the customer determine the **need** for your product?
- How will the customer **find out** about your product? 
  (at launch, before the customer has used your product)
- How will they **acquire** your product?
- How will they use the product, perceive value and receive support?
- How will he pay for the product?
- How will he spread awareness about the product?
  (after customers have used the product)
- What are the barriers for adoption?
### High Level Visual Specification
- **def:** A summary/brochure content about the *benefits* and features of the product.
- Helps customers gain consensus about the product and remove misunderstandings.
- It isn't final; treat it as a **baseline** on which to iterate and refine. 
### Quantify the Value
Quantify the value customers get from your product. 
Value generally falls into three categories:
1. better
2. cheaper
3. faster

It can be quantified by comparing the **as-is** and **to-be** states.
### Validate the Assumptions
You'll need to connect with the ==top 10 customers== in your [[C3 Market Segmentation#Beachhead Market|Beachhead Market]] and *validate* the assumptions you have made, be it with respect to purchasing priorities, needs, value propositions etc.
