## What is Philosophy?
- The study of fundamental questions about reality, human existence, knowledge, values, and beauty.
- from Greek meaning "love of wisdom"
## National Education Policy (NEP)
-  1988: Malaysia had its own education philosophy, the "**Falsafah Pendidikan Negara**". 
- 1896: Revised to "**Falsafah Pendidikan Kebangsaan**".

> Education in Malaysia is a continuous effort towards further developing the potential of individuals in a comprehensive and integrated manner. This is done to create a balanced and rounded human being in terms of intellectual, spiritual, and physical aspects based on faith and obedience to God. This effort is done to produce Malaysians who are knowledgeable, skilled, virtuous, responsible and able to achieve personal well-being., as well as contribute to the harmony and prosperity of their family, society, and country.
### Philosophical Features of NEP
Aims to: 
- produce balanced human beings
- produce graduates with a high level of humanity, virtue, and balanced, becoming competent
- have physical, emotional, spiritual and intellectual balance
## National Principles and Prosperity Philosophy
### National Principles
- malay: *rukun negara*
- declared on **31st August 1970** to commemorate the 13th independence of Malaysia
- introduced after 13th May 1969 incident that weakened racial unity

1. Belief in God (Kepercayaan Kepada Tuhan)
2. Loyalty to the King and Country (Kesetiaan kepada Raja dan Negara)
3. Supremacy of the Constitution (Keluhuran Perlembagaan)
4. Rules of Law (Kedaulatan Undang-undang)
5. Courtesy and Morality (Kesopanan dan Kesusilaan)
### Prosperity Philosophy (Sejahtera)
Six dimensions in **microcosm**:
1. Spirituality
2. Physico-psychological
3. Intellectual
4. Cognitive
5. Ethical
6. Emotional

Four dimensions in **macrocosm**:
1. Cultural
2. Ecological
3. Economic
4. Societal

Note that Prosperity Philosophy and National Principles only work if one understands the [[#Philosophical Features of NEP|NEP]] first. (NEP -> RN -> PP)
