## Western and Eastern Philosophy

| Eastern                                                         | Western                                             |
| --------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------- |
| Collectivism                                                    | Individual Focus                                    |
| Spiritual & Mysticism                                           | Hands-on style                                      |
| finding true selves in the long run and ridding of false selves | find meaning in life here & now with self at center |

## General Development of Philosophy
- **Religious/Spiritual**
  noble life & humanity
- **Natural**
  understanding the world, both physical and immaterial
- **Intellectual/Problem Solving**
  finding solutions to philosophical problems
### Timeline
1. **Ancient Greece**
   Discovery of Reason
2. **Christianity Revelation**
   new dyanism in thought
3. **Early Modern Period**
   new conception on reason & rationalist project
4. **Post Modern**
   crisis of reason due to critique on early modern period
5. **Contemporary (current)**
   attempts to address crisis of reason
## Theoretical and Practical Branches of Philosophy
### Theoretical
Requires deep intellectual endeavour to answer questions that may help in understanding the "*why*", "*how*" direction of human thoughts. 

Branches:
- **metaphysical**
  What does reality mean?
- **epistemology**
  What is knowledge?
- **psychology**
  Why do human beings have mental issues?
- **aesthetics**
  How can we define beauty?
### Practical
Philosophical discussions in *specific fields* which has specific & clear applications.

Branches:
- **Politics** 
  What does it mean to be a good government?
- **Economy**
  How to practice justice in allocation of economic wealth?
- **Ethics**
  How to maintain good ethics.
- **Civilization**
  How did the Islamic Empire fall?
## Emergence and Development of New Philosophical Branches
### Detractors
- **Jean Jacques Rousseau**
	- progress of Sciences & Arts have added *nothing* to our true happiness
	- our souls have become *corrupted* to the extent of the advancement of our Sciences & Arts toward perfection
	- luxury, dissolution, and slavery have been the *punishment* in every age
### 19th Century Worldview
19th century inventions like the railroad, lightbulb and the airplane were a cause of celebration, and people developed a new worldview.

- nature was **imperfect** and human reason could **improve** it
- deep belief in human reason to understand & to command nature
- technology would lead to **peace**
  *ex: invention of telegraph*
- improvements on nature
  *ex: addition of more species to remove invasive pests that threaten crops*
### 20th Century Changes
- world wars
- poverty
- inequality
- job loss
- economic upheaval
- concentration of wealth & power
- resource consumption
- pollution
### New Philosophers
- Bertrand Russell (1872 - 1970)
- Noam Chomsky (1928 - Now)
- Karl Popper (1902 - 1944)
### New Branches
- **Ecology**
  Study on consequences of industrial activity.
- **Organisation**
  Concept of work-life balance.
- **Technology**
  Idea of overdependence on technology.
## Summary
In summary, philosophy had always asked questions about the purpose of life. The eastern and western world had their own differences in philosophy but still had the objectives. The evolution of philosophy had given birth to theoretical and practical philosophies. With massive developments in the world, philosophy once again had emerged with new branches that may help us better understand and manage the emerging issues.
