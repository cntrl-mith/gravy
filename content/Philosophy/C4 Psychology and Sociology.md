## The concept of man through the perspectives of Philosophy and Religion
### The Concept of Man in Philosophy
The concept is **broad** since it investigates the wholeness of human existence.

It is an inquiry into the what-who-and-why of humans, including:
- characteristics that *distinguish* humans from non-humans
- characteristics that entitle an entity to be a *person*
- the philosophical *purpose or goal* of being human and an ideal human existence
### The Meaning of Life
Meaning in life can be essentially be found through:
- **communication**
  Being able to connect with a loved one or successfully conversing with someone through a language barrier.
- **understanding**
  Mapping and making sense of the world around us.
- **service**
  Helping other people.

### Religious and Civilizations Perspectives
#### Chinese
**Buddhism**
- Life is suffering.
- Man must understand reality & act on it to be free as nothing is permanent & perpetual as all things change. 
- One must attempt to be free from being dependent on impermanent ideas & objects.

**Confucianism**
- Focuses on human behaviour and maintaining 5 happy relationships.
- Principles of ethics must be followed to attain happiness. 
- Education helps in building character & good ethics.
- A virtuous life is worth living.

**Taoism**
- Concept of "Wu Wei" to take actions in accordance with nature.
- *Balance* is key and man must be able to maintain the relationship with nature & strive to understand man's place in the universe.
- Being *selfish* goes against the concept.
#### Indian
**Hinduism**
- Man is a creation that has a soul (atman), which is a spark from the divine (Brahman).
- Karma, rebirth, reunited with Brahman.
- Concept of man is perceived in physical and metaphysical perspectives.
#### Islamic
**Islam**
- man is a creation that has a *purpose*— to worship Allah SWT
	- Hablun min Allah (relationship with Allah)
	- Hablun min Annas (relationship with Humans)
	- Hablun min Al Alam (relationship with environment)
- all man came from Adam a.s.
- man is consisted of *physical being* and *spiritual* (heart, soul & intellect)
#### Western
**Classical Era (Greek)**
- "Man is a rational animal"— Aristotle 
- Rational thinking is a gift exclusively bestowed upon man.

**Middle Era (Christianity)**
- Man is God's creations & must obey God's commandments.
- Central virtue in life is love.
- All of man's roles, attributes and ethics are based on Christian teachings.

**Modern Era (Renaissance)**
- Different views on man were proposed.
- Some initially went against Classical & Christianity, but later went to support & improve on the classical ideas based on scientific findings.
#### Western vs Easter
**Western**
- Rational
- Intellectual
- Scientific

**Eastern**
- Spiritual
- Metaphysical
- Religious

## The concept of balance man from aspects of JERI
The Philosophy of National Education, JERI is a concept of a **well-balanced human being**, consisting of:
- Jasmani (physical fitness)
- Emosi (good emotional health)
- Rohani (spirituality, strengthen relationship with God)
- Intelek (ability to think, generate ideas and present arguments)

The **aim** is to create a:
1. good and honorable person
2. trustworthy and responsible citizen
3. loyal and enthusiastic citizen
## Summary
The concept of Man from various philosophy aims to achieve harmony and happiness in life. Human beings have more or less the same nature, but their external contexts are different from one another. This statement reflects to the discussion of concept of man in civilizations and religions’ perspectives. Before we conclude this part, if we understand JERI, which is a human being who has a holistic and balanced nature, then a human being is able to face any challenges. Unfortunately, the challenges he/she faces usually arise from one aspect of JERI; whether the challenge is physical, emotional, spiritual or intellectual.
